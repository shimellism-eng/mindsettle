Mindsettle

A calmer day, one thing at a time. An iPhone app for ADHD brains.

  • Today: what's next, and when to leave.
  • Dump: say everything on your mind; it becomes sorted reminders.
  • Settle: start one thing, park a worry, ride out a hard moment, move your body.

Everything stays on your iPhone. No accounts, no tracking.
