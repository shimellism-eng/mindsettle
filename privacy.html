Mindsettle

Privacy policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

Mindsettle keeps everything on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no adverts and no tracking. Nothing you type or say is sent to us.

What the app uses, and why

  • Calendar (read only): to show your day on Today, work out when to get ready and leave, and set your alerts.
  • Reminders: to save the tasks you dump and tick them off. These live in Apple's Reminders app, so they follow your own iCloud settings, the same as any reminder you add yourself.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: only after you tap the mic. Your voice is turned into text on your iPhone, with Apple's on-device recognition. Recordings are not kept.
  • Apple Intelligence: on supported iPhones, sorting your brain dump and replying to a worry are done by Apple's model on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us or to any AI company.
  • Health: Mindsettle does not use Apple Health at all.
  • Notifications: get-ready, leave and focus alerts, created on your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

Your name (if you give one), your support contact (if you choose one), your morning settings, parked worries and impulses, check-ins, and your lists are stored only in the app on your iPhone. Worries you say out loud are never saved as reminders. Deleting the app deletes all of this.

What we receive

Nothing, in normal use. If you test the app through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send, under Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

Mindsettle is not aimed at children under 13.

Not a medical service

Mindsettle is a self-help tool. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. If you are in crisis in the UK or Ireland, call Samaritans on 116 123, or 999 in an emergency.
